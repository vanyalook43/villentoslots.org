RTP, volatility, and game math

The library's mathematical framework is based on probability and statistics and uses clear and transparent principles. RTP is the theoretical percentage a game is designed to return for millions of plays. RTP does not predict how a player will do in the short term, but it is an important metric to understand how a game is designed to behave over a long time frame. Volatility describes a game's risk profile; a low-volatility game will have more frequent and smaller fluctuations, while a high-volatility game will have fewer results more spaced apart and greater variability between those results.
